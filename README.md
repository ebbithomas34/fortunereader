## What is FortuneReader?

FortuneReader helps you discover your ideal career through honest self-reflection. Instead of asking "help me find a career," it guides you through a structured assessment that generates a professional Claude.ai prompt based on your authentic preferences.

The tool recognizes that every career involves sacrifices:
- Outdoor work means weather exposure
- Solo work means isolation  
- High-stakes decisions mean stress

FortuneReader helps you identify which sacrifices you're willing to make to do work you can live with for years.

---

## How It Works

**3-Stage Assessment:**

1. **RIASE Selection** - Choose your core work style (Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, or Enterprising)
2. **Preference Differentiators** - Answer YES/NO questions about work conditions, environment, and demands
3. **Job-Specific Questions** - Review specific career matches

**Iteration Encouraged:**

Each circuit takes only seconds. Experiment with different YES/NO combinations to find what really resonates. Session history tracks your iterations so you can compare choices.

**Claude Integration:**

When you find a career you can live with, FortuneReader generates a focused prompt and opens Claude.ai for a serious conversation about your future.

## Usage

1. Click "Begin Assessment"
2. Answer YES/NO questions honestly
3. Review career matches
4. Try different combinations to explore options
5. When ready, copy the prompt to Claude.ai for discussion

**Pro Tip:** Don't aim for perfection on the first try. Iterate 3-5 times to understand the trade-offs and find what truly fits.
